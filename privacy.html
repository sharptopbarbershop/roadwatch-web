Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · applies to the RoadWatch Alerts iPhone app and the RoadWatch website.

Short version: camera alerts are computed on your phone. RoadWatch does not store your driving history or continuous location, does not sell data, and does not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Information we collect

  • Account: first and last name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and the date you accepted the Terms.
  • Content you submit: camera reports (camera type, the location you chose, optional direction, notes and photo), STILL THERE / NOT THERE votes, direction votes and correction proposals.
  • Subscription status: purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Your RoadWatch account identifier is attached to the purchase so it can be restored to your account.

Location

  • Proximity detection runs on your iPhone, including in the background when you enable it, so alerts work alongside Apple Maps or Waze.
  • To load cameras near you, the app sends an approximate position (rounded to roughly 100 m) to our server. Speed-limit lookups send only a ~2 km map-tile identifier. These requests are not stored with your account and we keep no location history.
  • A camera you report is stored at the location you chose for that report.
  • The on-device diagnostic log (used for field testing) stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What other people see

Community camera reports are shown on the map as “RoadWatch community member” without your name or contact details. Moderators can see which account submitted a report in order to handle abuse.

How we use information

To provide RoadWatch (accounts, camera data, alerts, community verification), to prevent fraud and abuse, to moderate content, and to contact you about your account. We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (database, authentication and file storage, United States) and Apple (App Store purchases). Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap, and website address search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim; those requests reveal the map area or search text you use to those providers. Camera and road data come from public government sources and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app at Profile → Account → Delete account. This deletes your profile, sign-in and photos. Camera reports you made stay in the public map data without any link to you. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple ID settings.

Children

RoadWatch is not directed to children under 13 and requires an account holder who is a licensed driver or of driving age.
