Common questions

Alerts don’t play in the background. In iPhone Settings › RoadWatch, set Location to Always (or While Using with background activity) and allow Notifications. Turn up media volume: spoken alerts use media volume.

Subscription. Start, restore, change or cancel RoadWatch Pro in the app (Profile › Subscription) or in your Apple ID settings. The web map is free.

A camera is wrong or gone. Open the camera on the map and choose NOT THERE, report the wrong location, or propose a corrected direction. Moderators review changes.

Report abuse or block a contributor. Open the report on the map and choose Report or Block.

Delete my account. In the app: Profile › Account › Delete account.
