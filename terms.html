What RoadWatch is

RoadWatch is a roadway-awareness service. It shows the reported locations of roadway cameras (for example traffic, speed, red-light, toll and license-plate-reader cameras) from public sources and from the RoadWatch community, and can alert you when you approach one. Information may be incomplete, outdated or wrong, and no alert is guaranteed.

Drive safely and lawfully

Always obey traffic laws, posted speed limits and the instructions of law enforcement. RoadWatch is not intended to help anyone evade law enforcement. Do not interact with your phone while driving; answer verification prompts only when it is safe and legal to do so.
